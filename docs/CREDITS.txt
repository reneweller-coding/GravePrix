GRAVE PRIX – credits
====================

Game, code, cars, tracks, texts, sounds and pictures: made for this game. All characters, drivers, cars, places and
firms are invented; any likeness to real persons or businesses is a coincidence.

Built with
- raylib 5.5 (zlib/libpng licence), https://www.raylib.com
- Barlow typeface by Jeremy Tribby (SIL Open Font License 1.1, see assets/fonts/Barlow-OFL.txt)

The music was generated locally with ACE-Step 1.5. Every sound effect and engine is synthesised by the game; when a
rival dies the band plays the opening bars of Frédéric Chopin's Funeral March (Piano Sonata No. 2, 1839, public
domain), synthesised as well.
Ricky Rattle's voice was made locally with Qwen3-TTS (voice design and cloning, an invented voice).
The tabloid's press photos were painted locally with FLUX.2 [dev]; they show invented people only.
