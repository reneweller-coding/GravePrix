GRAVE PRIX
==========

A top-down combat racer with a career. Mortimer Graves, owner of the funeral group Graves & Sons, runs an
underground racing league and earns twice from every crash: from the bets and from the funerals. You start in the
lowest of five leagues with a rusty car and little money. Race, shoot, mine, ram, earn, upgrade, climb league by
league, beat each league's champion in a showdown, and at the top race Graves himself on his estate.

Starting
--------
Windows:      GravePrix.exe
Linux/Deck:   ./GravePrix   (on the Steam Deck: add it to Steam as a non-Steam game, it runs natively)

Your settings, career and records live in %APPDATA%\GravePrix (Windows) or ~/.local/share/GravePrix (Linux).

Modes
-----
Career       five leagues, race days with three offers, garage, black market, loan shark, sponsors, the tabloid
Quick race   any track and car; race, derby (last car driving wins) or time trial

Difficulties
------------
Five, from gentle to merciless: they change the computer drivers' pace, skill, temper and love of weapons, the
damage you take and deal, prize money, prices, repairs, how often wrecked rivals die, and the loan shark's rate.

Tips
----
- Wrecks stay on the track and burn. Do not drive into them.
- Rivals who are wrecked may die. Their seat goes to a newcomer. Champions are harder to kill.
- Cash, wrenches, ammo, nitro, mines, rockets and coffins (anything can be inside) lie on the track.
- Sabotage on the black market leaves a rival's car half broken for the next race. Do not get caught.
- The loan shark is patient. Until he is not.
