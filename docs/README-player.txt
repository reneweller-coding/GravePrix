GRAVE PRIX
==========

A top-down combat racer with a career. Mortimer Graves, owner of the funeral group Graves & Sons, runs an
underground racing league and earns twice from every crash: from the bets and from the funerals. You start in the
lowest of five leagues with a rusty car and little money. Race, shoot, mine, ram, earn, upgrade, climb league by
league, beat each league's champion in a showdown, and at the top race Graves himself on his estate.

Starting
--------
Windows:      GravePrix.exe
Linux/Deck:   ./GravePrix   (on the Steam Deck: add it to Steam as a non-Steam game, it runs natively)
macOS:        ./GravePrix   (untested on a real Mac; the first time right-click it and choose Open)

Your settings, careers and records live in %APPDATA%\GravePrix (Windows) or ~/.local/share/GravePrix (Linux, macOS).

Modes
-----
Career       five leagues, race days with three offers (and now and then a job for Graves), garage, black market,
             loan shark, sponsors, the tabloid
Test drive   eight short lessons on the Graves & Sons yard, Ricky Rattle explaining: throttle, brakes, handbrake,
             guns, mines, nitro, rockets (from the title screen, and first on a new career's paddock)
The crypt    65 epitaphs to earn over all your races and careers, each a gravestone (title screen and paddock)
Three career slots; the credits from the title screen (and after the final).
Quick race   any track and car; race, derby (last car driving wins) or time trial; or a random track
Endless tournament   a new random track every round; finish in the top three to race on
Daily challenge      the same random track and car for everyone today, a time trial against your ghost

Difficulties
------------
Five, from gentle to merciless: they change the computer drivers' pace, skill, temper and love of weapons, the
damage you take and deal, prize money, prices, repairs, how often wrecked rivals die, and the loan shark's rate.

Options for everyone
--------------------
Driving assist (on by default): the car turns only as sharply as its tyres can hold and stays planted until you
really push it; the handbrake still lets you drift. Turn it off in the options for the slippery handling of the old
top-down racers.
Text size, race display size, colour vision (red-green or blue-yellow: other colours for good and bad), names
above the cars, frame rate (30, 40, 60 or unlimited; 40 saves battery on the Deck), vibration, camera height,
screen shake, the announcer's lines. With a pad the hints and the controls screen show its buttons. With a colour
vision setting the rivals race in colours that stay apart for red-green and blue-yellow eyes. On the Steam Deck the
game starts in the fast graphics setting (smaller shadow map, lighter bloom, 67 % resolution); 40 frames save battery.

Graves' jobs
------------
Some race days bring a fourth card: a job for Mortimer Graves. No league points, but he pays on top of the prize:
- The coffin: one on your roof. Reach the finish with at most 40 % damage, or it falls off.
- The debtor: a rival owes Graves money. Wreck their car yourself.
- The hearse: the Graves & Sons hearse races with you. The rivals go for it; see it home.
- The wreaths: nine lie on the track. Pick up six before the others run them over.

Upgrades have a price on the road
---------------------------------
A stronger engine makes the rear twitchy on full throttle (lift off and it bites again), soft tyres cost a little
top speed, armour weighs (slower off the line and on the brakes, but harder in a crash). The garage shows what the
next level gives and takes.

Tips
----
- Level crossings ring before the train comes. The train does not brake. Drawbridges rise for the ships.
- On the pass, rocks fall where the ground glows. The weather can turn in the middle of a race.
- Wreck a rival twice and you have a feud: they will come for you. Kill them and they get a stone in the
  Graveyard of Honour, with their last words.
- Dolores sells a used car most days, cheap and a third broken. Graves pays for the move up a league.
- Each league's champion brings their own monster to the showdown.
- Wrecks stay on the track and burn. Do not drive into them.
- Rivals who are wrecked may die. Their seat goes to a newcomer. Champions are harder to kill.
- Cash, wrenches, ammo, nitro, mines, rockets and coffins (anything can be inside) lie on the track.
- Sabotage on the black market leaves a rival's car half broken for the next race. Do not get caught.
- The loan shark is patient. Until he is not.
- Bins, cones, crates, grave crosses and snowmen stand on the shoulders. Knock them flying; a hydrant spouts.
- The dead do not always rest: on the cemetery's tracks and at the final, the last rivals to die race along as
  ghosts. They cannot hurt you. They still talk.
- Sign a sponsor and their name goes on your roof.
- Stuck in a league? Its showdown asks fewer points with every season you spend there. And every final Graves wins
  takes a little out of him.
