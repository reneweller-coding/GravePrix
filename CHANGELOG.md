# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

- **Modelled cars:** the Rustbucket, the Tin Can and Brain Freeze are modelled properly now (Blender, by script)
  in today's design language: one smooth body that flows into the greenhouse, raked flush glass, slim LED lamps,
  integrated bumpers, flared arches and a little bigger wheels. The Rustbucket is a beaten-up modern compact with
  its primer bonnet and bumper, rust, rotten sills, odd door, taped tail light, roof rack with spare tyre and rope,
  one mirror and the coat-hanger aerial; the Tin Can a two-tone city car with its belt line, rolled canvas roof and
  wind-up key; Brain Freeze an ice-cream van on a current van with its hatch, candy awning, sign, speaker and cone.
- **New steering for every car:** the nose turns quickly at any speed and the tyres decide how fast the car follows.
  Turn harder than they grip and the car slides and scrubs speed off in the bend instead of running wide into the
  wall: a U-turn at full throttle from 108 km/h is 22 m wide instead of 39 m, held full lock circles on two car
  lengths, and a slide settles as soon as you let go of the key. The handbrake drift, ice, oil and mud stay slippery.
- **Brake help:** a third level of the driving assist lifts the throttle and brakes by itself when the car is too fast
  for the bends ahead. It only ever takes speed away; your own brake works as always.
- **Assist off is for experts now:** the same tyres as the computer drivers, more drift. Old settings keep their choice.
- **The computer drivers** steer and plan their bends for the new tyres and corner faster; they no longer get stuck
  on posts in the road. The career stays as hard as it was (Graves drives a touch slower at the final).
- **The camera looks ahead:** it leads your car the way it drives, and further when you drive down the screen, where
  the tilted view sees less: at 108 km/h 61 m of road ahead up or down the screen (56 and 34 m before), 84 m across
  (66 m). It never turns, and a spin or a crash glides instead of jerking.
- **Corner arrows:** the next bend at the top of the display, its shape as seen from above, how far and how sharp
  (one to four marks, green to red), and the bend right after it. The messages in the middle move to the bottom while
  you drive down the screen. Option "Corner arrows" (on); not in arenas.
- **Steering that points the way:** with a pad the car can head where the left stick points on the screen, whichever
  way it faces (option "Steering: stick points the way"; the default still turns the wheels). Backing up, the nose
  comes round to the stick as well.
- **Back on track:** stuck against a wall or turned round? Backspace, T or the pad's Select/View puts the car back on
  the racing line a few metres back, standing, facing the right way; it blinks and passes through the other cars for
  a moment, and the next one waits five seconds. The display offers it when you stand stuck.
- **Miniature look:** the top and the bottom of the picture blur like a photograph of a model, with a sharp band
  round your car (Options > Miniature look; lighter on the Steam Deck).
- **Every venue has its own colours:** cold blue night at the cemetery, teal and orange in the docks, bleached noon
  in the desert, neon violet downtown, a cold storm light at the estate.
- **Things that stand sit on the ground:** soft contact shadows at the foot of walls, buildings, gravestones,
  trees and parked cars, darker at night.
- **Shadows from above:** now and then the Graves & Sons airship drifts over the track towing its banner (read the
  shadow), or the Sensenblatt's news helicopter follows your car, its rotor's shadow sweeping over you; at night
  its searchlight is on you. Both can be heard overhead.
- **Lightning over the estate:** the storm's flashes light the whole scene and throw the shadows for a moment;
  sometimes a bolt strikes beside the road and scorches it, and the thunder comes a moment later.
- **Real spectators on the stands:** seated and standing fans in team colours, scarves, caps and little flags, next to
  mourners in black with top hats and umbrellas, instead of coloured blocks. The stand's roof now covers only the back
  rows.
- **The crowd comes alive:** fans jump up and raise their arms when cars race past, a wave runs along the stands now
  and then, camera flashes go off at night, flags flutter.
- **Confetti and fireworks** at the line when the winner and when you finish.
- **Your sponsor's name on the stands' roofs** (Graves & Sons' slogan on every other one, and in quick races).
- **The podium:** after a race the first three stand on a pile of coffins below the results, the crowd cheering,
  Graves and Ricky Rattle beside them; a derby's runners-up smoke as the wrecks they are.
- **Wind:** trees, reeds and flags sway, gentle on clear days, harder in the rain, wildly in the estate's storm.
- **The junkyard's oil pools** shimmer in soft bands along their edges instead of a grid of coloured dots.
- **The options in four tabs:** picture, sound, display and driving (Q/E or LB/RB), each setting with a line of help.
- **The racing line** (option, display tab): a band on the road ahead, green where the car may stay on the throttle,
  yellow where to lift, red where to brake, worked out from your car's grip and brakes and the rain.
- **The tyres warn you** (option, driving tab, on): a thin singing just before they slide, and a soft hum in the pad.
- **Game speed** (driving tab): the whole race at 70 to 100 % pace for more time in every bend; best times count
  only at 100 %.
- **The driving school** (driving tab): three exercises on the proving ground with the racing line on: a slalom
  through cone gates, hairpins at the end of long straights, and two strings of esses. Bronze, silver and gold, a
  best time each, and an epitaph for three golds.
- **The crowd can be heard:** the stands roar when you come near, cheer when the field races past, gasp at a crash
  and applaud your finish.
- **Graves & Sons collect the wrecks:** ten seconds after a car is wrecked the hook comes down from their airship and
  lifts it away (not in a derby). Marshals wave yellow flags beside it until it is gone.
- **The replay** (option, on): after a race the finish (or your wreck) once more in slow motion from cameras at the
  track. Enter skips it.
- **The tabloid prints your podium:** after a career race the front page shows the real podium of that race in
  newsprint.
- **Two players on one screen** (quick race: race or derby): side by side, each with a camera and display of their
  own; the second player needs a pad.
- **Sharper stands:** the miniature look's sharp band widens to take in a stand in the picture.
- **The news helicopter is seen:** it hangs beside your car at the picture's edge, its lights blinking at night, its
  searchlight a cone of light in rain and fog.
- **Benchmark** (picture tab): half a minute on the heaviest track with and without the miniature look; it sets the
  look and, if needed, the graphics. The miniature look's new setting "automatic" (the default) turns it off by itself
  when the frame rate does not hold in a race.

## [1.0.1] – 2026-11-02

Steering you can lean on.

- **Full lock turns tight:** with the driving assist, held full lock makes the front tyres scrub the speed off until
  the turn fits, instead of drawing ever wider circles with the throttle down (a U-turn at full throttle from
  72 km/h: 33 m instead of 87 m). The tyres hold a little more, also once they slide; the career stays as hard.
- **Reversing lights:** white lights while backing up (hold the brake at a standstill); the test drive says so.

## [1.0.0] – 2026-11-01

The finished game. The difficulty now rises over a career instead of falling, and the world reacts.

- **A gentler start, a steeper climb:** the rivals of the low leagues drive slower, those of the high leagues faster;
  a league that holds you for seasons asks fewer points for its showdown; Graves slows a little after every final
  he wins. Measured with a test driver that drives like a person at a keyboard (late, all-or-nothing keys).
- **The driving assist holds better** (the slide of a person's hands drops from 2.7 to 0.5 m/s).
- **Upgrades have a price on the road:** a stronger engine makes the rear twitchy on full throttle, soft tyres cost
  top speed, armour weighs. The garage shows what the next level gives and takes.
- **Jobs for Graves:** some race days bring a fourth card: carry a coffin on the roof, wreck a debtor, see the
  firm's hearse home, pick up the wreaths. Paid on top, no league points.
- **Things that fall over:** bins, cones, crates, grave crosses, garden gnomes and snowmen on the shoulders; hydrants
  spout.
- **Life by the road:** spectators throw themselves flat, birds take off (crows, gulls, pigeons, vultures, bats),
  yard dogs chase the field.
- **The estate's garden:** a hedge maze, the pool with a sunken hearse, a greenhouse, a gazebo, an army of gnomes.
- **Ghosts:** on the cemetery and at the final the last rivals to die race along, pale and talkative.
- **65 epitaphs** to earn, each a gravestone in the crypt.
- **The test drive** at the undertaker's: eight short lessons, Ricky Rattle explaining.
- **Three career slots** and the **credits** (from the title, and after Graves).
- Ricky talks about you: lap records, close wins, comebacks, revenge, sprees; more takes of his lines.
- The sponsor's name on your roof.
- Builds for Windows, Linux and (untested) macOS on GitHub's runners.

## [0.2.0] – 2026-10-31
