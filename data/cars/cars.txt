# The cars of the Grave Prix (docs/DESIGN.md "Cars"); their models are in data/models/cars.txt.

# brick that shrugs off a hail of bullets, one goes for top speed. Every class is clearly faster than the one below.
#
# Note on the order of lines: "upgrades engine=.. armor=.. gun=.." comes before "engine", "armor" and "gun", because
# a block's named values and its lines' first words share one table (the later one wins).
# "length" and "width" stand on lines of their own for the same reason.
