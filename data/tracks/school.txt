# The driving school on the proving ground (App::startSchool, docs/DESIGN.md "The driving school"): three exercises,
# one lap each, alone against the clock with the racing line on. "school" gives the medals' times in seconds (the
# time with the penalties): bronze, silver, gold. Not raced in the career or the quick race (test_ names).

# 1. Slalom: gates of two cones, now left, now right; a missed gate costs 2 s, a cone knocked over 1 s.
track test_school_slalom
  title "Fahrschule: Slalom" title_en "Driving School: Slalom"
  venue proving
  laps 1
  width 14
  shoulder 3
  start 0 0 90
  straight 330 surface=concrete
  right 180 r=30
  straight 330
  right 180 r=30
  close
  gate at=50 lat=-0.45 w=4.6
  gate at=80 lat=0.45 w=4.6
  gate at=110 lat=-0.45 w=4.6
  gate at=140 lat=0.45 w=4.6
  gate at=170 lat=-0.45 w=4.6
  gate at=200 lat=0.45 w=4.6
  gate at=230 lat=-0.45 w=4.6
  gate at=260 lat=0.45 w=4.6
  gate at=290 lat=-0.45 w=4.6
  school bronze=42 silver=37 gold=33
  place start_gantry x=0 z=0 rot=90

# 2. Hairpins: two long straights, each ending in a hairpin: brake while the line is red, power on at the apex.
track test_school_hairpin
  title "Fahrschule: Haarnadeln" title_en "Driving School: Hairpins"
  venue proving
  laps 1
  width 12
  shoulder 3
  start 0 0 90
  straight 260 surface=concrete
  right 180 r=12.5
  straight 260
  right 180 r=12.5
  close
  school bronze=29 silver=26 gold=24
  place start_gantry x=0 z=0 rot=90

# 3. Esses: two strings of bends, the second tighter; steer early and smoothly along the line.
track test_school_esses
  title "Fahrschule: Kurvenkombination" title_en "Driving School: Esses"
  venue proving
  laps 1
  width 12
  shoulder 3
  start 0 0 90
  straight 80 surface=concrete
  left 60 r=30
  right 120 r=30
  left 60 r=30
  straight 50
  left 60 r=20
  right 120 r=20
  left 60 r=20
  straight 85
  right 180 r=35
  straight 388.2
  right 180 r=35
  close
  school bronze=48 silver=42.5 gold=39.5
  place start_gantry x=0 z=0 rot=90
